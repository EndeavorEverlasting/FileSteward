What does “unsigned preview” mean?

This pre-alpha executable has no publisher signature. Windows may display a security warning. Check the source and checksum and follow your device’s security policy; signing and broad release acceptance are not claimed.

Sample and imported reviews are observation-only: the downloadable welcome app cannot rescan, approve actions, or delete files. Recurring cleanup and maintenance preferences are not available in this preview. A sample review is synthetic evidence, not proof of a scan, deletion, or reclaimed space on your computer.
